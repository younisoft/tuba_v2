# AI Opportunities

- Lead scoring based on channel, listing, response history, and engagement.
- Property description generation in Arabic and English.
- Image quality analysis: brightness, duplicate photos, room coverage, watermarking, blur.
- Duplicate listing detection across agency inventory.
- Pricing suggestions from neighborhood, property type, size, and competing listings.
- Smart credit recommendations: which listing should receive Hot/Signature/Refresh.
- Natural language search across listings, leads, credits, and staff.
- Smart notifications for stale leads, underperforming listings, and expiring licenses.
- Predictive analytics for expected views, clicks, and leads.
- Conversational assistant for agents and admins.
- Gamification scoring assist: Bayut's TruBroker badges (Quality Lister, Responsive Broker, Super Lister) already compute an Images Score and Features Score per listing — an AI layer could turn those into specific, ranked fix-it actions ("swap photo 3, it's blurred; add 2 more exterior shots to hit Quality Lister") instead of a locked badge with no path forward.
- License-risk forecasting: cross-reference FAL license "Valid until" dates against active/removed listings to predict revenue-at-risk before a license lapses (observed gap: Bayut shows the expiry date and the resulting "Ad License Expired" status as two disconnected facts).
- Draft-recovery assistant: for listings stuck in Draft with "Insufficient Credits", compute and suggest the minimum top-up (or reallocation from an underperforming listing's upgrade) needed to publish.
