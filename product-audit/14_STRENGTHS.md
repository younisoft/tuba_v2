# Strengths

- Strong marketplace fit: listings, credits, leads, and packages are tightly connected.
- Dashboard uses meaningful business KPIs instead of vanity-only stats.
- Listing table exposes operational and performance dimensions in one place.
- Credits history creates billing/spend transparency.
- TruLeads moves Profolio beyond listing management into conversion management.
- Bilingual and Saudi-market signals are present, including REGA license fields.
- Profile/TruBroker prompts encourage trust-building behaviors.
- TruBroker™ gamification (Leaderboard, TruPoints™, quality/responsiveness badges) is a genuine, differentiated mechanic — it converts otherwise-invisible listing quality and responsiveness into a status system tied to a real public trust badge.
- Removed listings retain their full lifetime performance history (views/clicks/leads), so an agency doesn't lose the record of what a lapsed listing achieved.
- Reports Summary adds a location-concentration view (per-district share of inventory) that isn't available anywhere else in the product.
