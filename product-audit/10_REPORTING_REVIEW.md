# Reporting Review

Reporting exists as dashboard metrics, listing-level performance columns, credits history, TruLeads trend controls, and a dedicated Reports → Summary screen (listings composition, location breakdown, tiered/purpose-filtered performance time series). The system provides solid operational reporting but limited management reporting: nothing found aggregates spend against outcome, compares periods, or benchmarks against the market.

What Reports Summary actually contains (captured this pass):

- Listings breakdown by purpose and tier (Active/For Sale/To Rent/Daily Rentals/Signature/Hot/Basic)
- Breakdown by location: per-district share, e.g. one purpose skewed 58.3% into a single district — useful for spotting inventory concentration risk, but presented as a flat list with no map
- Performance: Views/Clicks/Leads plus channel breakdown (Calls/WhatsApp/SMS/Emails), segmented by purpose, with a "Last 30 Days" range control and a daily time-series chart filterable by tier
- No export, no scheduled delivery, no saved report configurations found

Recommended reporting additions:

- Agency executive report: listing inventory, spend, leads, conversion, SLA — a true roll-up, not a mirror of the dashboard widget.
- Agent report: assigned leads, response speed, contact attempts, conversion — the raw ingredients already exist on Agent Performance, just not exported as a report.
- Listing quality report: completeness, media quality, price competitiveness, REGA/license state — ties directly to the TruBroker Images/Features Score already computed per agent.
- Credit ROI report: credit spend by boost type versus incremental lead output.
- License-expiry-impact report: which active/removed listings are tied to a license approaching its "Valid until" date (data exists on the Licenses screen but isn't cross-referenced anywhere).
- Exportable scheduled reports in CSV/PDF.
