### Draft Listings (My Listings → Draft tab)

- URL: https://profolio.bayut.sa/en/listings?f%5Bnested.platform_listings.status.slug%5D=draft
- Screenshot: ../screenshots/10-en-listings-draft.png
- Purpose: Hold listings that were created but not yet published, and surface why.
- Primary users: listing manager, agency owner.
- Observed UI: table with Property/Timeline/Status/Publish/Actions columns (no Performance column — nothing to measure pre-publish).
- Statuses observed: "Not Posted" (saved but not submitted) and "Insufficient Credits" (blocked on package balance).
- Outputs: Per-row "Publish Now" button; a listing blocked on credits still gets the same CTA, with no inline guidance on how many credits are needed or a shortcut to top up from the row.
- UX score: 6/10 — the two draft reasons are visually identical; a user has to already know their credit balance to understand why "Insufficient Credits" appears.
- Notes: Not modified/published during this audit (read-only capture).

### Removed Listings (My Listings → Removed tab)

- URL: https://profolio.bayut.sa/en/listings?f%5Bnested.platform_listings.status.slug%5D=removed
- Screenshot: ../screenshots/11-en-listings-removed.png
- Purpose: Retain lifetime record of listings that dropped off the active roster.
- Primary users: listing manager, agency owner.
- Observed UI: table with Property/Timeline/Performance/Status/Publish/Actions; lifetime Views/Clicks/Leads are preserved even after removal (one row showed 12,049 views / 231 clicks / 22 leads from a listing posted Nov 2025).
- Statuses observed: "Ad License Expired" and "Deleted" — both distinct from Draft's "Not Posted"/"Insufficient Credits", giving four total non-active states across two tabs with no single glossary explaining them.
- Outputs: "Deleted" rows still expose "Publish Now", implying deleted listings can be resurrected — not obvious that this is possible without visiting the tab.
- UX score: 6/10 — the historical performance data on removed listings is a strong asset (agencies can see what a lapsed license cost them) but it's buried in a tab most users would only open when hunting for something, not proactively.

### Agent Performance

- URL: https://profolio.bayut.sa/en/agent-performance
- Screenshot: ../screenshots/14-en-agent-performance.png
- Purpose: Gamify agent quality/responsiveness and gate a "TruBroker™" trust badge behind measurable behaviors.
- Primary users: individual agent, agency owner (via team table).
- Observed UI: Leaderboard rank ("Not Ranked"), TruPoints™ counter, Activity log link, "Unlock TruBroker™" checklist (Complete Profile / 2+ Active Listings / Active Bayut Package), three badge cards (Quality Lister, Responsive Broker, Super Lister — all Locked with sub-metrics: Images Score, Features Score, Calls Answered, WhatsApp Response, Active Listings), and a team performance table (Staff, Badges, Rank & TruPoints™, Quality Score, Responsiveness, Active Listings).
- Outputs: Per-agent scorecard; no export or historical trend for TruPoints™ visible on this screen.
- UX score: 7.5/10 — genuinely differentiated feature (turns quality-of-listing and responsiveness into a status game tied to a real trust badge), but every badge was Locked with no explicit "you need N more of X" countdown, so the mechanic is visible but not actionable.
- Correction: this is a distinct page from "Reports" — the original capture pass mis-scraped the two adjacent sidebar labels ("Agent Performance" + "Reports") as one nonexistent menu item and failed to load either. Both are captured correctly in this pass.

### Reports (Summary)

- URL: https://profolio.bayut.sa/en/reports/summary
- Screenshot: ../screenshots/15-en-reports-summary.png
- Purpose: Aggregate portfolio-level performance and composition analytics.
- Primary users: agency owner, operations admin.
- Observed UI: Listings composition breakdown (Active/For Sale/To Rent/Daily Rentals/Signature/Hot/Basic), "Breakdown By Location" with per-district share (e.g. Taiba District 58.3%, Al Salehiyah 8.3%), a Performance module mirroring the dashboard's tabbed KPIs (Views/Clicks/Leads plus Calls/WhatsApp/SMS/Emails) with an "All / For Sale / For Rent / Daily Rentals" segmented filter and a "Last 30 Days" date-range control, and a daily time-series chart filterable by listing tier (All/Basic/Hot/Signature).
- Outputs: No visible export (CSV/PDF) control on this view.

### Settings → Licenses

- URL: https://profolio.bayut.sa/en/user-settings/licenses
- Screenshot: ../screenshots/17-en-user-settings-licenses.png
- Purpose: Hold the agency's FAL (real-estate broker) license and its CR number, owner, and validity, as the compliance backbone for every listing REGA ID.
- Primary users: agency owner / license holder.
- Observed UI: single license card (License number, Verified badge, Agency, CR Number, contact phone/email, address, Owner name, "Valid until" date) and a "Share with agency staff" toggle.
- UX score: 7/10 — clear and trustworthy presentation of a compliance-critical record; the "Share with agency staff" toggle is the only real access-control primitive visible anywhere in the product, and it's binary (all staff see it, or none) rather than scoped to specific roles or specific listings.
- Note: this screen is where "Valid until" expiry lives — it directly explains the "Ad License Expired" status seen on Removed listings, but nothing here links forward to which listings will be affected as expiry approaches.

### Settings → Preferences

- URL: https://profolio.bayut.sa/en/user-settings/preferences
- Screenshot: ../screenshots/18-en-user-settings-preferences.png
- Purpose: Toggle a small set of account-wide behaviors.
- Primary users: agency owner, agents.
- Observed UI: three toggles — "Smart Credit Utilization" (auto-spend expiring credits on boosts), "Push Notification", "Image and Details Usage" (consent to let Bayut use the agency's photos/details in TruBroker™ marketing across TV and digital channels). All three were on by default at capture time.
- UX score: 6/10 — "Smart Credit Utilization" is an autonomous spending decision made on the account's behalf with no visible logic, threshold, or history of what it has spent; the marketing-usage consent toggle being on by default for a paid B2B tool is worth flagging in any consent/opt-in review.

### Notifications Panel

- URL: https://profolio.bayut.sa/en/dashboard (header bell)
- Screenshot: ../screenshots/20-en-notifications-panel.png
- Purpose: Surface lifecycle and engagement nudges without a page navigation.
- Primary users: all roles.
- Observed UI: dropdown panel, "Mark all as read", scrollable list of cards with icon/title/body/relative-timestamp. Observed items: two "New Lead Alert!" (WhatsApp lead, phone-call lead), "Upgrade your listings!" (cross-sell to Hot/Signature), "Complete Your TruBroker Profile", "Start Your TruBroker Journey".
